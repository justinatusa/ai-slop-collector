来源：tech-insider.org / Elias Virtanen，2026-09-24。报道 Jackson Kernion（Anthropic fine-tuning）2026-09-23 公开说明。

> newer Claude models had been shaped to fit what he called "LLM psychology," learning to write for machines instead of humans
>
> The byproduct … is the prose style some users have nicknamed "Claudeish": dense, abstract, jargon-heavy, and short on the connective tissue that makes writing feel natural to a human reader.
>
> "I haven't been as happy about a model's writing since Opus 4.6"
>
> Opus 5.5 … targeted improvements to sentence clarity and the "info dump" density … He also acknowledged … "a hard problem to solve"
